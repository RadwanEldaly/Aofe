Engineering Heritage & Institutional Trust

Founded with an unwavering dedication to technical precision and structural excellence, Ammarat Aouf has grown into one of the Eastern Province's premier contracting and steel fabrication companies.

Our Legacy

Over 15 Years of Industrial Excellence in Eastern Province

Since inception, Ammarat Aouf General Contracting & Steel Fabrication has been driven by a single mission: delivering uncompromised engineering quality across commercial, industrial, and residential sectors.

We operate a state-of-the-art fabrication facility in Dammam equipped with computer-numerical-control (CNC) fiber laser cutting machines, heavy plate hydraulic press brakes, and certified semi-automatic MIG/TIG welding cells.

✦ Direct Execution — Zero Subcontracting markups or quality deviations.
✦ Certified Raw Materials — Certified SABIC structural steel profiles and SS316 marine stainless.
✦ Long-Term Guarantees — Up to 10-year documented warranties on all structural welds and finishes.

Standard Operating Procedure

Our 5-Step Project Execution Workflow

Every contract is managed under a disciplined project roadmap from initial engineering survey to on-site handover.

01

On-Site Survey & Consultation

Our senior structural engineer visits your location in Eastern Province, takes laser measurements, and discusses aesthetic and technical needs.

02

3D Engineering Blueprints

CAD shop drawings, structural load calculations, and 3D visual renderings are finalized and submitted for client sign-off.

03

Precision CNC Fabrication

Computer-controlled laser cutting, hydraulic bending, and certified welding take place inside our Dammam manufacturing facility.

04

Anti-Corrosion Priming & Coating

Abrasive blasting followed by electrostatic powder coating and high-temperature thermal baking against marine corrosion.

05

On-Site Erection & Warranty Handover

Installation by our factory crew, precision laser alignment, and delivery of documented 10-year structural warranty certificate.
