Everything You Need to Know Before Starting

Engineered clarity on contract terms, steel thickness, anti-rust coatings, and installation guarantees.

For CNC laser-cut exterior gates, we use heavy solid steel plates ranging from 3mm up to 8mm thickness (never light 1mm commercial sheets that deflect over time). For industrial hangars and structural space frames, we strictly utilize certified SABIC I-beams, H-beams, and heavy structural tubes (SHS/RHS) certified under Saudi Building Code (SBC) standards.

We provide a comprehensive documented warranty of up to 10 years on all structural framing, load-bearing joints, and weld integrity. For surface finishes, our electrostatic oven-baked powder coating carries a 5-year guarantee against peeling, blistering, and discoloration under Gulf coastal humidity.
